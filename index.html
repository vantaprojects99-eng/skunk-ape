THE 20-YEAR HUNT

For two decades, GTA players have hunted a Bigfoot that wasn't supposed to exist. Every game, the trail got warmer. In Leonida, it finally leads somewhere.

2004

The San Andreas Conspiracy

Whispers spread through forums: a Bigfoot lives in the Back o' Beyond woods of San Andreas. Blurry screenshots. Fog at 3 AM. Players burn hundreds of hours combing Mount Chiliad. Nobody ever finds him.

THE DENIAL

Rockstar Steps In

The hunt gets so loud that Rockstar addresses it directly: there is no Bigfoot in San Andreas. The believers hear something else. You don't deny a myth that nobody's chasing.

2013

GTA V: "The Last One"

Hit 100% completion and a final hunt unlocks. Franklin tracks Bigfoot through the woods, takes the shot… and it's a man in a Bigfoot suit. A decoy. The real one was never in Los Santos.

THE LEAK

Legendary Animals

Leaked GTA 6 files reportedly list "legendary animals" to hunt across Leonida, and the name that keeps surfacing is the Skunk Ape. Florida's own Bigfoot. Unconfirmed. Unsurprised.
